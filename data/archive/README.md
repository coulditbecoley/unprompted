# Archive

Discontinued categories. The run data stays in `data/runs/` and is never
deleted: it was published, it was real, and this project's rule is that records
are added rather than removed.

## pokemon-grading

Measured once, week of 2026-08-21. 225 answers across ChatGPT, Claude and
Perplexity, 24 grading companies found, PSA named first in 44% of runs.

Discontinued 2026-08-22 when the publication narrowed to AI categories, where
the engines being queried also sell the products being ranked. That is the
measurement no competing tool can produce, and focus was judged worth more than
breadth.

Its question bank and alias map are kept here so the category can be revived
without rebuilding them.
