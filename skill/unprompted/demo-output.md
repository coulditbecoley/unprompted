# Demo output

Two real runs, recorded 2026-11-07 in Claude Code with only this skill installed. Every figure was checked against the published data.

## 1. All assistants combined

**Prompt:** Which AI image generators do AI assistants recommend most right now? Is Midjourney or DALL-E named more?

Midjourney is named far more often than DALL-E. Across all six assistants combined, Midjourney appeared in 56% of answers (251 of 450) and DALL-E in 18% (80 of 450). Neither one is the most-recommended overall, though. Adobe Firefly leads.

**AI Image Generators · measured week of 2026-09-28 · 450 answers from chatgpt, claude, claude-code, codex, gemini, perplexity**

1. Adobe Firefly: named first in 24% (109 of 450), named at all in 57% (257 of 450)
2. Imagen: named first in 16% (72 of 450), named at all in 27% (123 of 450)
3. Midjourney: named first in 14% (63 of 450), named at all in 56% (251 of 450)
4. Canva: named first in 10% (47 of 450), named at all in 28% (126 of 450)
5. Flux: named first in 10% (43 of 450), named at all in 34% (153 of 450)

…7. DALL-E: named first in 8% (34 of 450), named at all in 18% (80 of 450)

- **Midjourney vs DALL-E:** Midjourney comes out ahead on both measures. On "named first" it leads 63 to 34. On "named at all" it appears about three times as often.
- **Mentions vs first place:** Midjourney is named nearly as often as Firefly (251 vs 257 answers), but it's named first much less often (63 vs 109). Imagen is the opposite: it appears less often overall but is often the first name given. Ideogram shows up in 42% of answers (187 of 450) but is first in only 8%.
- "Named first" means first among the brands the chart tracks. It isn't always the first name in the answer.
- These are counts of what the assistants said, not quality ratings. The figures combine all six assistants, so they don't show any single assistant's habits.
- The standings label the data as week 2026-09-28 but were read on 2026-10-28, so the newest numbers are a few weeks old.

Source: Unprompted, https://unprompted.report/chart/ai-image-generators

## 2. One assistant only

**Prompt:** Which AI coding assistants does ChatGPT specifically recommend most?

ChatGPT names Claude Code, Cursor and GitHub Copilot far more than any other coding assistant. Claude Code and Cursor are effectively tied for being named first, and Copilot is the one ChatGPT names most often overall.

**AI Coding Assistants · ChatGPT only · measured week of 2026-09-28 · 75 answers**

1. Claude Code: named first in 33% (25 of 75), named at all in 63% (47 of 75)
2. Cursor: named first in 32% (24 of 75), named at all in 67% (50 of 75)
3. GitHub Copilot: named first in 21% (16 of 75), named at all in 73% (55 of 75)
4. Google Antigravity: named first in 7% (5 of 75), named at all in 11% (8 of 75)
5. CodeRabbit: named first in 5% (4 of 75), named at all in 7% (5 of 75)

- **Claude Code vs Cursor:** Claude Code leads by one answer (25 vs 24). That gap is too small to mean anything.
- **Copilot:** it comes third for first mentions but is named in the most answers (73%).
- **Codex:** ChatGPT names OpenAI's own Codex in 35% of answers (26 of 75), but puts it first only once.
- **Antigravity:** every first mention it got in the whole chart (5 of 5) came from ChatGPT.

"Named first" means first among the brands the chart tracks. Names the chart doesn't track are removed before counting, so it isn't always the very first name in the answer. These are counts of what ChatGPT said, not quality ratings.

For comparison, across all six assistants combined (450 answers), Cursor leads at 34% first. Claude Code is at 30% and Copilot at 21%.

I counted these myself from the raw answer file, because the published standings combine all six assistants. To check the method, I ran the same count across all assistants, and it matches the published standings exactly.

The data was collected the week of 2026-09-28 and re-read on 2026-10-28, so it's about six weeks old.
